## Defects found by the reviewer, and the fix

- `LEDGER.md` listed `T-1103-13` twice: the round had a correction record appended with the
  same `task_id`, and the exporter wrote one row per log line. Fixed: the exporter now
  collapses by `task_id` (latest wins) and only then writes `ledger/<task_id>.json`.
- The index did not show `reply_sha256`. Fixed: the column is now present, together with
  the `note` column.
- `ts` was rendered in the local (US-style) date format because `ConvertFrom-Json` turned the
  ISO string into a `DateTime`. Fixed: the raw ISO-8601 string is preserved.

## What this spot check does NOT establish

The archived reply text is an OCR capture of the visible viewport (tail of the window), not a
complete transcript. Even if the hashes match, that only shows the archived bytes were not
altered after they were written. It does not show that the GUI actually sent exactly these
characters, nor that the reply was captured completely. The reviewer's verdict therefore
stays **needs_review**, and the independent leg still requires a human to open the
conversation and compare.
